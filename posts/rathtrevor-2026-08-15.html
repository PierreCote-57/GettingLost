Our first trip without shore power, at BC Park Rathtrevor, near Parksville, lots of learning.

The park: Beautiful! The campsites are a bit wider than those at the earlier commercial campgrounds, but we are still close to many other campers. Yet, we are in the woods, with only wilderness behind us. Later, we will explore the park, walking to the beach.

When I picked-up the van, the battery was dead. Turns out the guy on the web was correct (go figure!) and our salesman was not: The power cutoff button is not enough while storing the van. It appears "to be understood" that, in this mode, the battery will drain in about 1-2 weeks (it did). Crawling under the van to physically disconnect the battery (negative post) is not realistic. There seems to be two possible solutions

  1. Keeping the van plugged while in storage
  2. Have an aftermarket master switch installed

While neither option is ideal, we will go for plugged in for the near future.
