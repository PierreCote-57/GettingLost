First, the place was awesome, just as expected. At low tide, the beach really goes on forever.

To say the trip was a disappointement is an understatement. Issues encountered:

  1. Not enough power
  2. Terrible internet signal

It appears that, while we enjoy getting away, the beautiful environment, we also have our habits and our needs. It should not have been surprising that we wanted internet access, and found it difficult when the phones/Hotspot failed due to insufficent signal. Similarly, we appreciate some level of creature comfort and running out of battery power was an experience we are choosing to not repeat

Where does that leave us?

Internet

The internet thing is easily solvable with a Starlink. The van has an external connector for the Starlink Standard, which appears to terminate inside as a RJ45 connector. My understanding is that the dish's cable goes outside, the RJ45 inside goes in the router, in the van, and line voltage (120 VAC) goes to the router. Confusingly, the mobile van is equipped with a connector for the Starlink intended for a static permanent installation.

An alternative is the Starlink mini, That one is intended for nomads, highly portable and requiring less power. By default, it would plug into 120 VAC, but adapters exist to power it from a cigarette lighter plug (adapter has a 12 VDC-> 24 VDC adapter to avoid the low end of the range of acceptable voltage and reduce the current on the 15m cable.

The Starlink mini seems like the right choice for us.
