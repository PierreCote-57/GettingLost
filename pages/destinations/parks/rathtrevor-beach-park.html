Road map

Rathtrevor Beach Provincial Park is on Highway 19A just south of Parksville, about two hours from Campbell River. The campground has 226 vehicle-accessible sites and 25 walk-in sites, with flush toilets and showers, and it fronts the long sandy beach the park is known for. Sites are reservable May to October; a reduced-service, first-come first-served winter season runs mid-October to mid-March.
