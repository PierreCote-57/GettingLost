The van has been upgrated with two SOK 206Ah | SK12V206H 12Volt LiFePO4 Battery giving it 412 Ah of available energy.

There are four (4) variables that are used in different configuration depending on the conditions:

  1. Shore power
  2. The Truma panel, left of the garmin control panel
  3. The Disconnect button, right of the garmin panel
  4. The Solar button, right of the garmin panel
