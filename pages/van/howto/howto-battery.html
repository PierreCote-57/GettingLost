The van has been upgrated with two SOK 206Ah | SK12V206H 12Volt LiFePO4 Battery giving it 412 Ah of available energy.

There are several variables/configuration depending on the conditions:

  1. Shore power, typically 30A circuit
  2. Generator (2.8 KW)
  3. Ford engine (aka ALternator)
  4. Inverter (2,000 W)
  5. Solar panels (220 W)
  6. The Truma panel, left of the garmin control panel
  7. The Disconnect button, right of the garmin panel
  8. The Solar button, right of the garmin panel
